Please read before starting

The passages introducing each set of questions ("stimuli") are invented. Claude wrote them to sound like a period document, but they are not real quotations from an actual historical source — don't cite them, and don't assume the wording reflects a real text.

This section is more error-prone than the rest of this study guide. Everything else in this tool is built directly from your teacher's own terms list and class notes. These questions are AI-generated to mimic the AP question style, so specific facts, dates, and especially the wrong-answer choices could be subtly off.

This is supplementary practice, not your primary MCQ prep. The best way to prepare for the actual multiple-choice section is still the Unit 1 Progress Check on AP Classroom and its feedback.
