Good to know

  • 10/12 on the SAQs and 10/12 on the MCQs = 100% on the test.
  • There are no bonus points on this test.
  • Students who qualify for extra time will be able to complete the test during the regular period. The test is 40 minutes long for regular timers.

SAQ Practice

These sets are modeled on real AP short-answer questions: SAQ 1 uses a historian’s argument, SAQ 2 uses a primary source, and SAQ 3 has no source. Each has parts a), b), and c). The test has two sets like these, 2 points per part. Read the verbs carefully: identify means name it; describe and explain mean give specific evidence and say how or why. Write your own answer first. Once you’ve written at least 20 words for a part, you can unlock ideas and sample answers to compare with. This site does not grade what you type.

Before you start: these practice questions are not the same quality as the questions you will see on the AP Exam, which are written and tested by experts. Use them as extra practice, but make the AP Classroom Progress Checks your priority. They are the closest thing to the real exam.
The source passages below were written by AI to sound like real sources. They are not real quotations, and the attributions are generic, not real authors. The Kongo letter is modeled on real letters by Afonso I, but it is not his actual wording. Sample answers are AI-written too, so they may not earn full credit. Practice MCQs

Twelve questions in four sets of three, the same number as the test, in AP format: a passage or table, then questions on argument, evidence, causation, and continuity. More than one answer may look true. Pick the one that best answers the question. Your goal on the test is 10 of 12.

Before you start: these practice questions are not the same quality as the questions you will see on the AP Exam, which are written and tested by experts. Use them as extra practice, but make the AP Classroom Progress Checks your priority. They are the closest thing to the real exam.

Please read before starting

  • The passages are invented. AI wrote them to sound like period sources and historians. Don’t cite them. The table uses rounded estimates from the Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database.
  • These questions are AI-generated, so a fact or a wrong-answer choice could be slightly off.
  • This is extra practice. The best multiple-choice prep is still the Unit 4 Progress Check in AP Classroom.

Review Race: Lisbon to Calicut

Follow Vasco da Gama’s 1497–98 route around Africa to India. Every correct answer sails your ship one leg. First ship to reach Calicut wins. The race takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
