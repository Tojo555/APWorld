Before you start

This isn't a test, and there are no right answers. It's a set of questions to help you see how the circumstances you were born into — the ones nobody chooses — compare to the rest of the world.

Nothing you enter here is saved or sent anywhere. This page runs entirely in your browser — there's no server, no account, and no record of your answers once you close the tab.

Please don't type in anything specific enough to identify you or your family — every question here can be answered with a broad category, not an exact number or name. That's on purpose.
